Check Board of Studies documents

Upload one BoS PDF, or several at once. Each is checked separately and up to {{ workers }} are checked at the same time. Checking takes roughly five seconds a page, because every page is read and examined for seals, stamps and signatures.

    PDF only. Hold Ctrl (or Shift) to pick several files. Up to {{ max_mb }} MB in total.

    What gets checked

    • Page 1 is on the college letterhead
    • The round seal appears on every page, first to last
    • Where the minutes end, the Principal and HOD have signed and sealed
    • Every agenda item is minuted, and matches what was listed
    • The attendance sheet is on the letterhead
    • There is a page recording who was present and absent; absent members have not signed the attendance sheet and present members have
    • Annexure-1 states the percentage modified (or Nil), signed and sealed
    • Every course in Annexure-1 has its syllabus attached in Annexure-2
    • Odd-semester BoS: result analysis, with graphs and the round seal
    • Even-semester BoS: feedback from students, alumni, teachers and employers
    • An Action Taken Report, signed and sealed
    • Even-semester BoS: the feedback graphs are signed and sealed

    Odd or even semester is worked out from the meeting date: a meeting held July–December reviews the odd semester, January–June the even one. Rules that do not apply to this semester are marked as such rather than failed.
